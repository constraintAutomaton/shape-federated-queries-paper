\section{Shape-Guided Federation}\label{sec:approach}

We propose \emph{Shape-Guided Federation}, an approach that replaces the costly custom summaries
used by HiBISCuS and FedUP with RDF data shapes, which are increasingly published alongside
SPARQL endpoints and offer greater expressiveness at no additional construction cost.
We first describe the content of the federation members with a shape index in \cref{sec:summaries},
then present \ac{SASS}, our shape-aware adaptation of FedUP's result-aware source-selection
algorithm, in \cref{sec:sass}.
We then compare the expressiveness of shape indexes with the summaries of prior approaches in \cref{sec:expressiveness}.
Finally, we discuss how shape indexes can be published by data providers or extracted by third parties to deploy Shape-Guided Federation in \cref{sec:deployment}.
